# Candidate literature for AuxMech Paper 2

This is an **unverified working library**, not the reference list of a published
review and not a set of included studies. Its records were consolidated from the
recovered review folder on 26 October 2026 without remote metadata verification.

## Files

- [Candidates 01](candidates-01.bib)
- [Candidates 02](candidates-02.bib)
- [Candidates 03](candidates-03.bib)

The three BibTeX files form one library of 102 candidate records with their
original keys. Entries are packed into the fewest files that fit the source limit.
They are split to meet the shared 500-line source limit. Both source bibliography
versions contained 103 entries; 35 differed only in journal abbreviation. The
full-name journal version was selected, so no duplicate figure-library copy is
needed. No duplicate DOI strings occur in the selected source library, but that
does not exclude different-version or title-based duplicates.

The Zotero `zotero-item-830` record titled “Inbox” is excluded from the BibTeX
files. It is not an identifiable work.
Six candidate records lack years; some lack venues and identifiers. Apparent
line-number contamination in two titles and possible Maurizi version duplicates
are flagged in per-entry BibTeX comments rather than silently corrected. No
separate CSV index is required. Incomplete article records are
exported as `misc` until their identity and publication type are checked.

Each entry carries an unverified-status note. Source abstracts, copyright fields,
keywords, access dates and source-manager metadata are omitted from the portable
metadata derivative; original files were preserved separately before relocation.

Parsing and a successful BibTeX build establish syntax and record coverage only.
They do not verify metadata, eligibility, reported findings or scientific quality.
The [published Paper 1 bibliography](../../../bibliography/README.md) is separate
and has its own reference-number keys and verification status.
