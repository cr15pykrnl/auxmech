confirmed manuscript title. The intended work reviews machine-learning research

## Available material

They have identical citation keys and differ only in full versus abbreviated
journal names for 35 records. The full-journal-name version supplies the
[single candidate bibliography](bibliography/README.md).

Of the 103 source records, 102 are retained as unverified literature candidates.
The remaining record is a Zotero “Inbox” container, not an identifiable reference.
Original citation keys are retained. Abstracts and source-manager fields are not
redistributed in the derived metadata files.

No manuscript, abstract, review extraction table, eligibility decisions, analysis
code or results were present in the folder moved into this repository. A candidate
bibliography is not evidence that the studies have been read, screened or included.
