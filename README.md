# AuxMech: detecting deformation mechanisms in auxetics

This work investigates how re-entrant honeycomb auxetics deform under loading.
The published study extends plastic hinge tracing with image processing,
K-means clustering and logistic regression. It reports four of six deformation
modes in its study data; generalisation to other geometries is not established.

### Abstract from the accepted manuscript

The intricate geometrical configuration of an auxetic structure enables high energy
dissipation capacity at the expense of a highly nonlinear mechanical response. Under
external stimuli, complicated deformation mechanisms emerge which dictate the
extent of energy dissipation. Recently, the new ‘plastic hinge tracing’ method (Dhari
et al. 2021) was introduced to detect such deformation mechanisms for elastoplastic
porous materials. This approach is however subjective and cumbersome since it
requires monitoring several plastic regions in consecutive deformed configurations.
The present study innovatively extends this method by implementing machine
learning (ML) techniques for objective detection of deformation modes in auxetics.
To this end, a logistic regression ML model was developed to classify the deformation
modes of a re-entrant honeycomb structure. The proposed procedure could
successfully detect four out of the six deformation modes (‘X’, distorted ‘X’, ‘V’,
and ‘V+Z+V’) using the training datasets generated by the finite element analysis
and image labels created by K-means clustering algorithm. The success of the
proposed automated approach lays the foundation for identifying the deformation
mechanisms of other auxetics and porous materials with plastic deformations.

## Published approach and scope

The study extends plastic hinge tracing to classify deformation patterns in
re-entrant honeycomb auxetics. Finite-element deformation images are processed
and grouped into eight K-means image classes; logistic regression predicts the
class, which is interpreted as a deformation mechanism. The paper reports four
of six named modes in its study: X, distorted X, V and V+Z+V.

This repository provides the paper link, citation and an editable
conceptual graphical abstract. The paper-matched implementation and dataset
have not been identified here, so this companion makes no clone-to-training or
reproduced-accuracy claim. Generalisation to other geometries remains unestablished.

## Related data

The separate [KNet2 partial-recovery dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/cr15pykrnl/knet2-auxetic-deformation-partial)
belongs to an unpublished neural-network extension. It is not the published
logistic-regression paper's reproduction dataset. Its card records missing inputs,
unverified annotations/units and split/version limitations.
